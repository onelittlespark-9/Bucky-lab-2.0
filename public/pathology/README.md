The pneumothorax source has no visible side marker; laterality is taken from its
published description. Its fictional clinical history is not used. The effusion
image already contains a black circle. The CT image is one slice demonstrating
intracerebral and intraventricular haemorrhage, not a full study. All three were
visually inspected before integration. No identifying case history is imported.

Captions and findings are brief educational summaries, not diagnostic reports.
General imaging signs were cross-checked against the linked Radiology Masterclass
pleural disease and intracerebral haemorrhage tutorials. Their images are not
copied. In-app credits link to each original image and its applicable licence.
