Data sources, licences, references & educational disclaimer

Educational use only. Bucky Lab 2.0 is a teaching simulator, not a medical device, diagnostic service or substitute for clinical imaging, local protocols, qualified supervision or professional judgement. Images may be source-derived, transformed, simulated or wholly synthetic and must not be interpreted as a real patient's diagnostic examination.

TotalSegmentator CT dataset

Some current anatomical HU volumes and segmentation-derived regional assets are derived from the TotalSegmentator CT dataset created by the Department of Research and Analysis, University Hospital Basel. Dataset DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.6802614. The dataset record requests citation of the associated TotalSegmentator work. Bucky Lab identifies transformed regional assets as derived rather than acquired examinations.

VSDFullBody / Virtual Skeleton Database

Higher-detail anatomy may be derived from the VSDFullBody collection: Kistler, Michael, VSDFullBody: The Virtual Skeleton Database Full Body CT Collection. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.8270365. The record describes anonymised postmortem whole-body CT scans and states a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike (CC BY-NC-SA) licence. Material derived from this source is therefore for non-commercial use, requires attribution, and applicable adaptations must be shared under the corresponding ShareAlike terms. Source-derived material will be identified in case provenance.

Generated and simulated content

Bucky Lab may generate educational anatomy, pathology, contrast enhancement, projections, interpolation and other imaging characteristics where suitable source data is unavailable. Such content is labelled synthetic or simulated in provenance. Generated content is not represented as acquired clinical data and does not inherit evidentiary or diagnostic validity merely because it was informed by external anatomical references.

Attribution and reuse

Each external dataset remains subject to its own licence and attribution requirements. Zenodo hosts records but does not itself grant a uniform reuse licence for all deposited content; reuse is governed by the licence shown on each record. Users wishing to reuse Bucky Lab source-derived assets should review the original dataset record and licence before redistribution or other use.
