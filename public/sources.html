Data sources, licences, references & educational disclaimer

Educational use only. Bucky Lab 2.0 is a teaching simulator, not a medical device, diagnostic service or substitute for clinical imaging, local protocols, qualified supervision or professional judgement. Images may be source-derived, transformed, simulated or wholly synthetic and must not be interpreted as a real patient's diagnostic examination.

TotalSegmentator CT dataset — currently used

Current source-derived anatomical HU volumes and segmentation-derived regional assets use the main TotalSegmentator CT dataset created by the Department of Research and Analysis, University Hospital Basel. Dataset DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.6802614. The main CT dataset is distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). This permits sharing and adaptation, including commercial reuse, subject to attribution and the other licence conditions. Bucky Lab identifies transformed regional assets as derived rather than acquired examinations and provides the source DOI and licence here. The associated work should also be cited: Wasserthal J et al. TotalSegmentator: Robust Segmentation of 104 Anatomic Structures in CT Images. Radiology: Artificial Intelligence. 2023;5(5):e230024. DOI: 10.1148/ryai.230024.

NLM Visible Human Project — approved high-resolution reference source

Bucky Lab has selected the US National Library of Medicine (NLM) Visible Human Project as the preferred higher-resolution whole-body CT reference instead of VSDFullBody. NLM describes the Visible Human datasets as publicly available/public-domain anatomical image resources and states that, since 2019, a licence is no longer required to access the datasets. The CT acquisition covers the entire body at 1 mm axial intervals with 512 × 512 images and 12-bit grey values. Source: NLM Visible Human Project; access information: Getting the Data. Bucky Lab will identify any derived regional volume as source-derived and acknowledge NLM and the Visible Human Project. External material embedded in or associated with the dataset remains subject to any separately identified third-party rights.

VSDFullBody / Virtual Skeleton Database — not incorporated

VSDFullBody was evaluated but is not being incorporated because its Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike terms are unnecessarily restrictive for Bucky Lab's intended distribution and protection model. Dataset DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.8270365.

Generated and simulated content

Bucky Lab may generate educational anatomy, pathology, contrast enhancement, projections, interpolation and other imaging characteristics where suitable source data is unavailable. Such content is labelled synthetic or simulated in provenance and is not represented as acquired clinical data. Content that constitutes an adaptation of licensed source material remains subject to the applicable source terms.

Attribution and reuse

Each external dataset remains subject to its own terms and attribution requirements. Users wishing to redistribute source-derived Bucky Lab assets must review the identified original dataset and applicable terms. No source attribution implies endorsement by the source authors or institutions.
