Data sources, licences, references & educational disclaimer

Educational use only. Bucky Lab 2.0 is a teaching simulator, not a medical device, diagnostic service or substitute for clinical imaging, local protocols, qualified supervision or professional judgement. Images may be source-derived, transformed, simulated or wholly synthetic and must not be interpreted as a real patient's diagnostic examination.

TotalSegmentator CT dataset — currently used

Current source-derived anatomical HU volumes and segmentation-derived regional assets use the main TotalSegmentator CT dataset created by the Department of Research and Analysis, University Hospital Basel. Dataset DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.6802614. The main CT dataset is distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). This permits sharing and adaptation, including commercial reuse, subject to attribution and the other licence conditions. Bucky Lab identifies transformed regional assets as derived rather than acquired examinations and provides the source DOI and licence here. The associated work should also be cited: Wasserthal J et al. TotalSegmentator: Robust Segmentation of 104 Anatomic Structures in CT Images. Radiology: Artificial Intelligence. 2023;5(5):e230024. DOI: 10.1148/ryai.230024.

Scope note: this statement applies to the main TotalSegmentator CT dataset used by Bucky Lab. Separately released TotalSegmentator subtasks or datasets are not automatically covered by this statement and must be licence-checked before incorporation.

VSDFullBody / Virtual Skeleton Database — candidate source, not currently incorporated

VSDFullBody is being evaluated as a possible higher-detail source: Kistler, Michael, VSDFullBody: The Virtual Skeleton Database Full Body CT Collection. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.8270365. The Zenodo record describes anonymised postmortem whole-body CT scans and states that the source data were shared under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike (CC BY-NC-SA) licence. The record does not clearly display a licence version in its Rights field. Because version-specific terms matter, Bucky Lab will not distribute VSDFullBody-derived assets until the applicable licence version/terms are verified or suitable permission is obtained. If incorporated later, the relevant material will be attributed, restricted to permitted non-commercial use, marked as adapted where applicable, and shared under the required compatible ShareAlike terms.

Generated and simulated content

Bucky Lab may generate educational anatomy, pathology, contrast enhancement, projections, interpolation and other imaging characteristics where suitable source data is unavailable. Such content is labelled synthetic or simulated in provenance and is not represented as acquired clinical data. Synthetic content created independently by Bucky Lab does not automatically inherit a third-party dataset licence merely because it models general anatomical or imaging principles; however, content that constitutes an adaptation of licensed source material remains subject to that source licence.

Attribution and reuse

Each external dataset remains subject to its own licence and attribution requirements. Zenodo hosts records but does not itself grant a uniform reuse licence for all deposited content. Users wishing to redistribute source-derived Bucky Lab assets must review the identified original dataset and applicable licence. No source attribution implies endorsement by the source authors or institutions.
