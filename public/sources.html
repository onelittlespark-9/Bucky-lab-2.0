Data sources, licences, references & educational disclaimer

Educational use only. Bucky Lab 2.0 is a teaching simulator, not a medical device, diagnostic service or substitute for clinical imaging, local protocols, qualified supervision or professional judgement. Images may be source-derived, transformed, simulated or wholly synthetic and must not be interpreted as a real patient's diagnostic examination.

TotalSegmentator CT dataset — currently used

Current source-derived anatomical HU volumes and segmentation-derived regional assets use the main TotalSegmentator CT dataset created by the Department of Research and Analysis, University Hospital Basel. Dataset DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.6802614. The main CT dataset is distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). Bucky Lab identifies transformed regional assets as derived rather than acquired examinations. Associated work: Wasserthal J et al. TotalSegmentator: Robust Segmentation of 104 Anatomic Structures in CT Images. Radiology: Artificial Intelligence. 2023;5(5):e230024. DOI: 10.1148/ryai.230024.

NLM Visible Human Project — approved higher-detail source; ingestion in progress

The U.S. National Library of Medicine describes the Visible Human Project as a publicly available, public-domain library of CT, MRI and anatomical cross-sections. The whole-body CT is 512 × 512, 12-bit, acquired at 1 mm axial intervals. NLM no longer requires registration or an annual licence to access the data, but downloading and redistribution remain subject to NLM Terms and Conditions. Bucky Lab must acknowledge the source conspicuously with “Courtesy of the U.S. National Library of Medicine”, must not imply NLM endorsement, and when redistributing data must either maintain the current data or conspicuously identify that the application may not reflect the most current/accurate NLM data. Source: NLM Visible Human Project — Getting the Data. Terms: NLM Terms and Conditions.

Implementation status: the importer and provenance pipeline are present, but the live regional CT assets have not yet been replaced by Visible Human-derived volumes. Bucky Lab will not label them as such until ingestion, anatomical coverage validation and deployment have completed.

VSDFullBody / Virtual Skeleton Database — excluded from application assets

VSDFullBody was evaluated as a possible higher-detail source: Kistler, Michael, VSDFullBody: The Virtual Skeleton Database Full Body CT Collection, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.8270365. Its CC BY-NC-SA conditions introduce non-commercial and ShareAlike obligations that are not compatible with the intended restricted Bucky Lab distribution model. It is therefore not incorporated into Bucky Lab application assets.

Generated and simulated content

Bucky Lab may generate educational anatomy, pathology, contrast enhancement, projections, interpolation and other imaging characteristics where suitable source data is unavailable. Such content is labelled synthetic or simulated in provenance and is not represented as acquired clinical data. Content constituting an adaptation of external source material remains subject to that source's applicable terms.
