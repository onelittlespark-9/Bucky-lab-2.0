Data sources, licences, references & educational disclaimer

Educational use only. Bucky Lab 2.0 is a teaching simulator, not a medical device, diagnostic service or substitute for clinical imaging, local protocols, qualified supervision or professional judgement.

Human Reference Atlas / Visible Human whole-body meshes — used in positioning

The radiography positioning view loads the male and female Human Reference Atlas whole-body GLB reference assemblies derived from the U.S. National Library of Medicine Visible Human data. The assets are provided by the Human Reference Atlas under CC BY 4.0. Male source: 3d-vh-m-united.glb. Female source: 3d-vh-f-united.glb. Attribution: Human Reference Atlas / HuBMAP; source anatomy derived from the Visible Human Dataset, courtesy of the U.S. National Library of Medicine.

BodyParts3D — selected source for complete musculoskeletal fill-in

BodyParts3D provides downloadable polygon anatomy meshes and is licensed CC BY 4.0. Required attribution: “BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International”. It is being used as the supplementary source for structures absent from the HRA whole-body assemblies, particularly complete appendicular and skeletal anatomy.

TotalSegmentator CT dataset — currently used for HU imaging

Current source-derived HU volumes and segmentation-derived regional assets use the main TotalSegmentator CT dataset, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.6802614, distributed under CC BY 4.0. Bucky Lab identifies transformed regional assets as derived rather than acquired examinations.

NLM Visible Human Project

The U.S. National Library of Medicine provides publicly available complete male and female CT, MRI and anatomical cross-sections. Bucky Lab acknowledges the source as Courtesy of the U.S. National Library of Medicine. Visible Human Project data access.

Generated and simulated content

Educational pathology, contrast enhancement, projections and other imaging characteristics may be simulated where source data is unavailable. Such content is labelled simulated and is not represented as acquired clinical data.
